This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • Contact details — name, email, shipping address, phone if you give it.
  • Order and membership history — tier, renewal dates, shipments, skips.
  • Payment information — handled entirely by Stripe. We receive only the last four digits, card brand, and expiry. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Basic site analytics — pages viewed and referring source, in aggregate.
  • Correspondence — emails you send us.

Why we use it

  • To make and ship your candles.
  • To bill your membership and send receipts.
  • To tell you what was chosen each month and when your box ships.
  • To answer your messages.
  • To detect and prevent fraud.

Who we share it with

Only the services required to run the business, and only what they need:

  • Stripe — payment processing and billing management.
  • [SHIPPING CARRIER / FULFILMENT] — to deliver your order.
  • [EMAIL PLATFORM] — to send membership emails.
  • [ANALYTICS PROVIDER, if any] — aggregate site statistics.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Email

Membership emails — shipping notices, billing receipts, scent announcements — are part of the service. Marketing emails always contain a one-click unsubscribe, and unsubscribing never affects your membership.

Cookies

We use only what is necessary to keep the site working and to understand aggregate traffic. We do not run third-party advertising trackers.

Retention

We keep order and billing records for as long as required by tax and accounting law (generally seven years). Marketing contact data is kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it.

Your rights

Wherever you live, you may ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or stop emailing you. Email us and we will respond within 30 days.

California residents have additional rights under the CCPA, including the right to know, delete, and opt out of sale — we do not sell personal information. Residents of the UK and EEA have rights under UK GDPR and GDPR, including portability and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Children

Sacred Scents is not directed at anyone under 18 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS and payment data never touches our servers. No system is perfect, but we hold as little as possible on purpose — the best way to protect data is not to have it.
