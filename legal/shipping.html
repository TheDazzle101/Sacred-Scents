Tracking

Every shipment includes tracking, emailed the moment your box leaves us. If tracking has not moved for five business days, contact us and we will investigate or re-ship.

Skipping a month

You may skip any upcoming month from the billing portal linked in your receipts, provided you do so before your renewal date. You will not be charged for a skipped month and your membership resumes automatically the following cycle.

Cancellation

Cancel at any time from the billing portal or by emailing us. Cancellation stops all future charges. A cycle already charged and poured will still ship to you.

Damaged or wrong items

Candles travel in glass. If anything arrives broken, photograph it and email us within 14 days of delivery. We will replace it or refund it — your choice — and you do not need to send the broken item back.

Returns

Because every candle is poured to order in a batch sized to that month's membership, we cannot accept returns of opened or burned candles.

Unopened candles may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a refund of the product price. Email us first for a return address. Return shipping is the customer's responsibility unless the item was damaged or sent in error.

If you simply didn't like the scent

Tell us. Discovery is the whole premise of this membership, and we would rather hear that a scent missed for you than lose you quietly. We read every message, and it directly informs what survives the Selection Table next cycle.

Lost or undeliverable packages

If a package is returned to us as undeliverable because of an incorrect address, we will re-ship once the address is corrected; re-shipping costs may apply. Please keep your shipping address current in your account before the 25th.
