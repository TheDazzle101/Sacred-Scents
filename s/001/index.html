The Making

I.

The testing shelf

Every cycle starts here. [300] scents are poured into test tins, cured, burned and judged side by side — for throw, for the melt pool, for how the room feels an hour later. Most never leave this shelf.

II.

The choosing

One survives. It becomes the only scent poured that month — and once this drop ships, it is retired for good. [A line about why this scent won.]

III.

The pour & the seal

Hand-poured in small batches. Each candle is crowned with a sacred-geometry seal, cast in contrasting wax and set dead-center and perfectly flush with the top — so the surface reads as one seamless piece.

IV.

The burn test

Before a single one is sold, the batch is lit and watched — an even melt pool, a clean, steady flame, and the seal melting into the pool the way it should.
