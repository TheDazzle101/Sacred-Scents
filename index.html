We sample

The Selection Table

Why one

A catalogue is a compromise.

Most candle companies ask you to scroll. Forty scents, all in stock, all forever. Nothing is chosen because nothing has to be.

We do the opposite. Every cycle begins with hundreds of candidates and ends with a single survivor — the one that held up hot and cold, at hour one and hour thirty, in a small room and a large one.

When it ships, it is finished. The mould is broken. What you burn in March cannot be re-ordered in June, and that is the point: a scent you cannot replace is a scent you actually pay attention to.

  • Hand-poured in small batch — never mass-produced to stock
  • Formulated in-house, not bought off a fragrance-house shelf
  • Retired permanently the month it ships
  • A decade of pour experience behind every batch

The Maker

Ten years of pouring. Longer with volatiles.

Sacred Scents is built on a decade of hand-pouring candles that have sat on shop shelves and sold out — and, before that, years of technical work in botanical extraction.

That background matters more than it sounds. Extraction is the study of how delicate aromatic compounds behave under heat and pressure: what survives, what flattens, what turns bitter. It is the same question a candle asks every time you light it.

Most candles smell best in the jar. Ours are formulated for hour three, in a real room, with the door open — because that is when anyone is actually paying attention.

Questions

Before you commit.

What if I don't like the scent that month?

You can skip any month from your account, as long as you do it before the 25th. You will not be charged and nothing will ship. Your membership picks back up the following cycle.

That said — the discovery is the product. Members regularly tell us their favourite candle of the year was one they would never have picked off a shelf.

When am I charged, and when does it ship?

You are charged on the same date each month when your membership renews. Orders are cut off on the 25th, poured in the days after, and shipped during the first week of the following month.

Join today and your first box goes out in the first week of next month.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, in two clicks, from the billing portal linked in every receipt we send. No phone call, no email chain, no offer you have to decline three times. If a membership needs a retention script to survive, it was not worth having.

Can I buy a past scent again?

No. When a cycle closes, that scent is retired permanently. Occasionally we re-release a single archive scent for existing members at The Altar tier and above — but it is never guaranteed and never sold publicly.

What are the candles actually made of?

Hand-poured in small batch using a clean-burning natural wax blend, with a fragrance load tuned per scent rather than per recipe. Full specifications — size, wax, wick, burn time — are listed at checkout and printed on The Scent Letter in every box.

Do you ship internationally?

We ship across the United States with tracking included on every membership. International shipping is opening in stages — write to us and we will tell you when your country is next.

Can I gift a membership?

Yes — take The Vigil or The Altar and have the extra candles sent with a gift note at no charge. For a full standalone gift membership, write to us and we will set it up by hand.
